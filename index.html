READ THE FAQ
FAQ

Before you enter.

Is THE BLACK LEDGER just a planner?

No. Its positioning is a 365-day record of discipline, execution and self-mastery. It is built around the loop COMMAND → EXECUTE → RECORD → SCORE → CONFRONT → CORRECT → RETURN.

Is it digital or printable?

Both. The Consumer Edition is intended for digital reading/navigation. The Print Edition is A4-native for printing and handwriting.

How long does it last?

The system covers 365 days across four quarters and contains 1,005 pages in the final editions.

Do I have to be perfect every day?

No. The system includes confrontation, correction and return. A Protected Day exists for a genuine major disruption and does not require catch-up.

Is this medical, psychological, financial or professional advice?

No. THE BLACK LEDGER is a structured self-management and record-keeping product. Its customer documents state that it is not a substitute for professional advice and does not guarantee outcomes.

Where are licensing and refund terms?

The customer package includes dedicated License & Usage and Refund & Support documents. Final commercial links will be connected after the checkout platform is verified.
